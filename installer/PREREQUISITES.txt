MSH v0.1.1 is the first formal release candidate for client review. It has not
been publicly released or code-signed. Windows or antivirus software may show
an unknown-publisher warning.

MSH runs locally and does not upload microscope images, CSV, JSON, or scan data.

For Basler cameras, install the official Basler pylon Runtime/driver for the
camera interface before using camera features. This application does not modify
COM ports, motor parameters, scan bounds, camera serial numbers, or PFS profiles.

This review build includes the approved WBC-only detection model
`wbc_focus_yolov8n_e_20260611.pt`. It draws WBC bounding boxes but does not
classify WBC subtypes and must not be treated as clinical validation.
